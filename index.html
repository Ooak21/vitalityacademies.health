The Learning Center

Earn your PhD.

The Personal Health Doctorate is the Vitality curriculum: a guided path through the science of your own body, so you understand every decision and can hold your results for life. Self-paced, in your portal, and reinforced live at the Academy.

Module 01

How your body works

Metabolism, body composition, and what your InBody scan is really telling you. The foundation everything else builds on.

Module 02

Nutrition that lasts

Protein, macros, and meals that fit your real life. Built with a registered dietitian, not a one-size template.

Module 03

The habit science

Behavior change that survives travel, stress, holidays, and life transitions. The part most programs skip.

Module 04

Understanding GLP-1

What the medication does, how to use it well, and how to keep your progress as your plan evolves.

Module 05

Movement for your body

Training that protects lean mass and fits where you are, guided by your fitness coordinator.
